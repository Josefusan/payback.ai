# Prizes & Strategy

Total pool ≈ $69,750. A project can win **one Grand Prize + one Sponsor Prize** OR **one Honorable Mention + one Sponsor Prize**.

## Our target stack of prizes

| Slot | Primary target | Fallback | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grand / HM | **1st Place ($12k)** | **Best Use of PayPal + AI** or **Most Impactful** ($5k) | Autonomous back office is PayPal-central and AI-load-bearing; impact story is concrete for SMBs |
| Also in play | **Best Use of Agentic Commerce** ($5k) | — | Agent actually moves money: sends invoices, pays vendors via Payouts, responds to disputes — gated by policy |
| Sponsor | **Best Use of AG Grid** ($5k / $2k / 3×$1k) | Best Use of APIMatic (3×$1k + 6mo sub) | Ledger/journal/reconciliation is the most natural AG Grid use case imaginable; 5 winners; AG Grid judge on panel |

Rationale for AG Grid over Render/Bryntum/Channel3: biggest pool, five winners, and an accounting product *is* a data grid. Requirement (sponsor page, verbatim): "This award covers both what your dashboard looks like and how you build it. Show us polish: custom widgets, theming, and layout tools, not just AG Grid or AG Charts on their own. Take Studio Agent Framework for a spin: a custom agent talking to Studio's built-in agents, or a swapped-in harness." → Use **AG Studio** with custom widgets + a custom "Controller" agent in the Studio Agent Framework.

Use **APIMatic Context Plugin** while building the PayPal integration anyway (free month of APIMatic Basic; keeps APIMatic prize as fallback). Say so in README + video.

Optional, only if time: Bryntum Calendar for the month-end close checklist ("agent that drives the schedule itself"), Elastic for semantic search over ledger/receipts. Do not dilute: one sponsor prize can be won.
