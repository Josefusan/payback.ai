# About the Challenge & Inspiration (from the Devpost overview)

## About the challenge
PayPal already moves money for millions of people and businesses every day, worldwide. This hackathon opens that platform up and asks what happens when you put AI behind the wheel — new agents, new ways to pay and get paid, tools nobody's built yet.

"Build What's Next with PayPal and AI" is a global online hackathon for developers, designers, founders, students, and builders. **No prescribed problem statements and no tracks.** Build an agent, app, automation, payment experience, business tool, social product, or something new.

**The only fundamental requirement: the project must meaningfully use both PayPal and AI.**

Your project should:
- Meaningfully integrate at least one PayPal technology, API, SDK, product, or developer capability
- Meaningfully incorporate AI into the experience or functionality
- Demonstrate a working prototype or proof of concept
- Be sufficiently documented for judges to understand and evaluate it
- Comply with the Official Rules

New or existing projects are allowed, as long as meaningful progress happens during the hackathon.

## Get started
1. Get a PayPal Developer account (5 min): https://developer.paypal.com/
2. Review example projects and brainstorm (30 min): https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/resources

## Inspiration themes (PayPal's examples)

**AI Agents or Automation** — agents/workflows that use PayPal to *take action* on a user's behalf — not just recommend, but transact. E.g., an agent that shops and checks out; a scheduling assistant that pays contractors when a job is marked complete; two agents negotiating and settling a payment (agent-to-agent commerce).

**Money Movement Experiences** — faster/simpler/more flexible money movement. E.g., send money with just a phone number; a freelancer dashboard of all payments across clients; instant gig-worker payouts on task approval; split a dinner bill by scanning a receipt; a fundraising page splitting one donation across causes.

**Business Solutions** — tools for merchants, freelancers, creators, platforms, marketplaces. E.g., multi-currency invoicing for freelancers; creator tipping/memberships; marketplace checkout that splits between platform and sellers; POS add-on for small merchants.

**Consumer Experiences** — easier to pay, get paid, or track money. E.g., budgeting app auto-categorizing PayPal transactions; "request money" embedded in chat; simpler checkout for students, families, seniors.

**Social Impact** — expand access or drive good. E.g., accessible donation platform; microloans/fundraising for the underbanked; nonprofit automating recurring payouts to field partners; neighbors pooling money for a solar co-op or cleanup fund.

> Our idea sits in **Business Solutions + AI Agents/Automation**: it *acts* (invoices, payouts, dispute responses) rather than only recommending — which is exactly the "not just recommend, but actually transact" framing PayPal used.
